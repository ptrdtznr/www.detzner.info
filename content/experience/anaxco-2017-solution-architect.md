#### [Anaxco GmbH](https://www.anaxco.de/)
##### January 2017 - November 2017

During my role as a **Software Solution Architect** I focused on optimizing and increasing the efficiency of Microsoft Dynamics AX 2012/2016 and Microsoft SQL Server through detailed performance analysis of source code and the integration of automated testing. 
These efforts resulted in significant improvements in system performance, reliability, and overall operational efficiency.

I also contributed to the software development of the [transport management system](https://www.anaxco.de/tms/), where I developed scalable and robust solutions to streamline logistics and enhance operational workflows. 
My work involved aligning technical development with business objectives, ensuring the delivery of high-quality, impactful solutions. 
These experiences have honed my skills in system architecture, performance optimization, and software development, laying a strong foundation for my success in roles such as Software Solution Architect.
